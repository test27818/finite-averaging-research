      <span class="abstract-full has-text-grey-dark mathjax" id="2608.20028v1-abstract-full" style="display: none;">
        Let $B_{m}$ and $D_{n}$ be the denominators of the $m$th and $n$th convergent of the real numbers $α$ and $β$, respectively. We introduce an abnormal method in the theory of simultaneous Diophantine approximation to the pair $α,β$. Namely, the question of finding simultaneous approximation is turned into study of small solutions of a linear Diophantine equation $xB_{m} + yB_{m+1} = zD_{n} + vD_{n+1}$. The Thue-Siegel&#39;s lemma guarantees the existence of a non-zero integer vector $(x,y,z,v)$ in such a way that $|x|,|y|,|z|,|v|$ are bounded above by $\big( B_{m} + B_{m+1} + D_{n} + D_{n+1} \big)^{1/3}$. For a class of numbers, including particular equivalent numbers $α$ and $β$, we show there exist a real number $κ=κ(α,β)&gt;1/2$ and infinitely many explicitly constructible positive integers $q$ such that $\|qα\| \le \frac{1}{q^κ}$ and $\|qβ\| \le \frac{1}{q^κ}$. As the result improves Dirichlet&#39;s theorem on simultaneous approximation it also confirms the classical Littlewood conjecture for such a pair $α,β$. In addition, we present general criteria for the classical Littlewood conjecture as well as for its <span class="search-hit mathjax">$p$</span>-<span class="search-hit mathjax">adic</span> counterpart.

For these embeddings, the Hausdorff dimensions are calculated and it is shown that the fractal measure on the image of $\Qp$ coincides with the Haar measure on $\Qp$. It is proved that under certain conditions, the image of the <span class="search-hit mathjax">$p$</span>-<span class="search-hit mathjax">adic</span> solenoid is an invariant set of <span class="search-hit mathjax">fractional</span> dimension for a dynamic system. Computer drawings of some fractal images are presented.
