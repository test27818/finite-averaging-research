                           Abstract
                           In the theory of congruence subgroups, one usually shows that, under suitable assumptions, the
                           normal closure of the mth power of an elementary unipotent matrix coincides with the full con-
                           gruence subgroup mod m.
                             For applications, it is sometimes useful to study the subgroup generated by the mth powers of
                           the elementary unipotent elements. We give an elementary proof for the fact that in SLn Z for
                           n  3, this subgroup is normal in a suitably defined congruence subgroup of SLn Z. Moreover,
                           the two subgroups coincide.

                              During a visit to Bielefeld, Professor A. Janner raised the following ques-
                           tion, in the context of an investigation in cristallography:
