Examples

Ann* Math* Search for the expressions in all fields. Abbreviations are also possible.
jt:''Annals of Mathematics'' Search for the exact journal title phrase.
jt:annals pu:mathematics Search for journal title containing the sequence annals and publisher containing the sequence mathematics.
sn:0003-486X Search for ISSN. Both electronic and print ISSN are accepted.
se:2531 Search for the exact serial identifier. This excludes homonyms. Compare to jt:Annals of Mathematics.
jt:Annals cc:05 Search for journal title containing the sequence Annals and classification code of its main field (first two digits of MSC code).
jt:Annals (cy:CN|RO) Search for journal title and country published in (here either China or Romania in two-digit ISO 3166-1 standard codes).
tp:b Search for serials of type book series.
tp:j st:o t Search for serials of type journal which are in the state open access and currently indexed cover-to-cover.
tp:c r Search for serials that are cited and have references.
py:1800-1899 la:no Search for 19th century serials which published in Norwegian.

Fields

any Includes cc, cy, jt, la, li, pu, sn
cc Main fields of the serial (given by first two MSC digits)
cy Country of the publisher (use name, e.g., cy:France, or ISO 3166-1 codes, e.g., cy:FR)
jt Title, acronym or short title
la Languages of indexed documents (la:French, or ISO 639-1 codes, e.g., la:FR)
li External link (URL)
pu Name/location of the publisher or editorial institution
py Time period the serial has been active (interval search with “-”)
se zbMATH serial ID
sn International Standard Serial Number (ISSN)
st State: open access (st:o), currently indexed (st:v), indexed cover to cover (st:t), is cited (st:c), has references (st:r)
tp Type: journal (tp:j), book series (tp:b)

Operators

a & b Logical and (default)
a | b Logical or
!ab Logical not
abc* Right wildcard
ab c Phrase
(ab c) Term grouping
