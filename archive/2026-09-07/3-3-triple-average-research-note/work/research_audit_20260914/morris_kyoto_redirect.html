Notice: Former European Mathematical Information Service (EMIS) Website

You have attempted to access a page from the former European Mathematical Information Service (EMIS) website. EMIS was a pioneering online platform that served as a central hub for electronic math resources in Europe and the homepage of the European Mathematical Society (EMS) from 1995 until its eventual integration into other services.

Over the years, the services provided by EMIS have been gradually integrated into other platforms, resulting in the eventual discontinuation of this website. If you are looking for information on mathematics or the European Mathematical Society, we recommend visiting the following resources:

  • The Homepage of the European Mathematical Society is available at https://euromathsoc.org/.
  • The Electronic Library of Mathematics (eLibM) has been fully integrated into zbMATH Open. You can access former eLibM serials through their zbMATH Open profile pages:
    • Archivum Mathematicum
    • Documenta Mathematica
    • ELA. The Electronic Journal of Linear Algebra
    • ETNA. Electronic Transactions on Numerical Analysis
    • Electronic Journal of Differential Equations (EJDE)
    • Journal of Algebraic Combinatorics
    • Journal of Graph Algorithms and Applications
    • Journal of Integer Sequences
    • SIGMA. Symmetry, Integrability and Geometry: Methods and Applications
    • Séminaire Lotharingien de Combinatoire
    • Theory and Applications of Categories

We appreciate your interest in EMIS and hope that you find our recommendations helpful. For further questions please use our contact form.
