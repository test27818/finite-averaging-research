Greg Martin - Abstracts

When you click on the title of a manuscript in the list on the other half of the screen, an abstract of the selected paper appears on this half of the screen, as well as links you can use to download the selected paper.

Please let me know about any broken or incorrect links you encounter.

You can also download preprint versions of these manuscripts from the Los Alamos Preprint Archive.

Math symbols reality check: the following display

φiφjt = δi,j     (1 ≤ i, j ≤ n)

is supposed to look like the statement that n vectors (called phi) form an orthonormal system, or what TeX would produce from the following command:

\phi_i\phi_j^t = \delta_{i,j} \quad (1\le i,j\le n)
